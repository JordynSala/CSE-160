For the game I placed orbs around the map that the player can find and collect.
For the wow I gave the player the ability to turn on and off rain.
To add and remove blocks use + or = and  - or _
Use W, A, S, D to move around
Use Q and E to pan the camera
Click and drag on the canvas to rotate the camera
