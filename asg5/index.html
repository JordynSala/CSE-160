Project Info

Jordyn Salazar jobsalaz@ucsc.edu

Controls: WASD move, Space up, Shift down, Q/E turn, drag mouse to look around.

The island and cliffs are made of cubes, the ocean is a plane, and the trees are made of spheres and cylinders

There is a animated sphere in the water that bobs up and down

For my lighting I have a hemisphere light with a sky color and a ground color, there is a spotlight in the light house that rotates, and there is a red point light on the boat

There is a sand texture, stone texture and grass texture for the island primary shapes

There are two custom textured 3d models, a boat and a lighthouse

There are over 20 primary shapes making up the scene

There is a skybox

Extra feature: There is a weather toggle, it turns off and on rain, fog and the lighthouse beam
